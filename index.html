ABOUT ME

I turn AI ideas into useful, real-world products.

I’m Babar Ali Khan, an AI Engineer based in Peshawar, Pakistan. I build practical applications using machine learning, LLMs, RAG, and AI agents to automate real business workflows.

My process begins with understanding the problem—not choosing the most complicated model. I select the simplest reliable approach, test it honestly, and turn it into software people can actually use.

My recent work includes DeliveryGuard AI, a late-delivery risk prediction system, and Mindsight, a machine-learning application for mental-health score estimation.

I’m currently focused on production-ready AI agents, RAG systems, and intelligent business automation.
